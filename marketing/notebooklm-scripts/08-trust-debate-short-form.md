# Short Form Video Script: The Viral Escrow & Trust Debate (TikTok / Reels)

**Target Platform:** TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts (Under 60 Seconds)
**Style:** Fast-paced, aggressive hook, split-screen debate style (one creator playing two characters, or text-to-speech AI voices with dynamic captions).
**Visuals:** High-energy, Alex Hormozi-style captions (bold colors, rapid pop-ins). Sound effects on key words (cha-ching, whoosh, ding).

*(Visual: Character 1 ("TradFi Broker") looking smug, holding a giant stack of rental applications. Character 2 ("Pabandi Builder") holding a phone with a glowing Pabandi logo).*
