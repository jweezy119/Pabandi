# Brag Plan: Pabandi V2 (Zero-UI Terminal)

## 9-Question Rubric

1. **What is the app?**
   Pabandi is a liquidity protocol and offramp for MENA/Pakistan where LPs fulfill trades entirely over WhatsApp and Telegram.
2. **What is the funniest or most impressive claim?**
   "Your new liquidity dashboard is your chat app."
3. **What is the visual hook?**
   Glowing neon cyan (`#00f0ff`) and purple (`#a855f7`) glassmorphism panels set against a deep `#02040a` space background.
4. **What should be shown from the actual UI?**
   A mockup of the WhatsApp/Telegram chat interface receiving a real-time intent broadcast, and the LP replying "ACCEPT".
5. **What is the shortest satisfying video?**
   20 seconds.
6. **What tone fits best?**
   **Tone Preset:** `cinematic` 
   **Direction:** Premium fintech promo (dark mode, sleek gradients, sharp cuts).
7. **What should the audio feel like?**
   Cinematic tech bed. Deep sub-bass drop on the reveal, with crisp UI "send" and "receive" ticks for the chat interface.
8. **What should the share caption say?**
   "We just launched the Pabandi V2 Zero-UI Terminal. Fulfill trades and earn yield without ever leaving Telegram."
9. **What's the user flow worth showing?**
   Intent broadcasts to LP -> LP replies "ACCEPT" -> Funds instantly settled notification.

---

## Storyboard (20 seconds)

**Audio Profile:** Deep cinematic synth pulse, ticking hi-hats, sharp UI sounds.

### Scene 1: The Hook (0:00 - 0:04)
- **Visual:** Pitch black. A single cyan cursor blinks. Then, a massive glowing headline fades in (Outfit font): "Dashboards are dead."
- **Motion:** Text scales down slightly (`cut-the-curve`), then whips off-screen left.

### Scene 2: The Reveal (0:04 - 0:08)
- **Visual:** A sleek glassmorphic panel slides up. Inside is a Telegram/WhatsApp chat view. 
- **Text (floating next to it):** "Meet the Zero-UI Terminal."
- **Motion:** The panel glides smoothly on the Z-axis.

### Scene 3: The Flow (0:08 - 0:14)
- **Visual:** Inside the chat, a message bubble pops in:
  *🚨 New Trade Request 🚨*
  *Send: 50,000 PKR*
  *Reward: $185 USDC*
- **Audio:** Crisp notification *ding*.
- **Visual:** A user chat bubble pops up instantly: `ACCEPT`
- **Audio:** Swoosh + *ding*.

### Scene 4: The Flex (0:14 - 0:17)
- **Visual:** The chat dissolves into glowing particles. A massive stat count-up fills the screen: `$500k+ Settled`.
- **Text (Bottom):** "Instant SLA enforcement. Built-in Mudarabah yield."

### Scene 5: Outro (0:17 - 0:20)
- **Visual:** The Pabandi logo drops in with a purple glow flare.
- **Text:** `pabandi.com`
- **Motion:** Fades to black.
