## 2. General

**Tagline / Elevator Pitch**
Sitara is the trust layer the $2.3T rental and service economy never had — a booking OS that secures showings, tables, chairs, and lease commitments with AI + escrow + on-chain review verification.

**One-line problem**
Service bookings are broken: tenants ghost viewings, restaurant tables sit empty after no-shows, salons lose 15–25% of revenue to ghosted appointments, and fake 5-star reviews make it impossible to tell who’s actually trustworthy.

A single booking platform with AI no-show prediction, automated escrow deposits, portable trust profiles, and on-chain review verification that makes fake reviews obsolete — from apartments to bottle service.

## 3. Product

**Core features**
- **AI reliability scoring** — predicts no-show probability before a booking is confirmed
- **Escrow-backed bookings** — deposit held in smart escrow, released on honored appointment
- **On-chain review verification** — every verified check-in mints a lightweight proof-of-attendance record; reviews tied to actual visits, not just accounts
- **Portable Sitara reputation** — trust profile travels across landlords, restaurants, salons, and nightlife venues
- **Rewards for reliability** — guests/tenants earn tokens for showing up, paying on time, and leaving honest reviews
- **White-label CRM + guest/tenant portal** — operators get branded booking and relationship workflows
- **Webhook integrations** — tenant.created → auto-screen, reservation.completed → release, sale.completed → reward
- **CSV/API import** — migrate from Buildium, AppFolio, Rent Manager, Yardi, Sevenrooms, Resy

**Verticals**
- **Apartments / rentals** — property viewing bookings, lease signing, deposit escrow, tenant screening
- **Restaurants** — reservation deposits, no-show protection, bottle service / event deposits
- **Salons & spas** — appointment deposits, stylist reliability, loyalty rewards
- **Nightlife** — bottle service bookings, guest list deposits, event commitments
- **Live selling** — stream-to-checkout with escrow-backed buyer commitment
- **Freelance** — scoped escrow for deliverables and milestones

**Marketplace mechanics**
- Map-powered discovery for local bookings
- Verified listings with Sitara reputation scores
- Cross-vertical: same trust layer works across rentals, services, and e-commerce

**Tech stack**
- Frontend: Firebase Hosting
- Backend: Node/Express, Prisma
- Blockchain: Solana (review verification proofs, escrow, rewards)
- Maps: Leaflet + OpenStreetMap
- AI: reliability/no-show prediction models
