# Brag Video Plan

## The 9-question rubric

1. **What is the app?**
   Pabandi is a Web3 PropTech and trust layer offering Zero-Knowledge Proof of Rent, Self-Sovereign Property DIDs, and Tokenized Yield Streams.

2. **What is the funniest or most impressive claim?**
   "Rent payments are instantly collateralized on Solana to accrue real-world DeFi yield, split 50/50 between tenant and landlord."

3. **What is the visual hook?**
   A glowing glassmorphism aesthetic using the `--primary` (`#00f0ff`) and `--secondary` (`#a855f7`) colors, contrasting against the deep dark surface (`#080e17`).

4. **What should be shown from the actual UI?**
   The ZK Proof of Rent wallet interface and the Yield distribution dashboard.

5. **What is the shortest satisfying video?**
   20 seconds. It takes time to reveal the crypto/DeFi features.

6. **What tone fits best?**
   Preset: cinematic. Creative direction: high-octane Web3 launch, glowing UI, big claims.

7. **What should the audio feel like?**
   Cinematic bed with a low swell, restrained motion-matched accents, and subtle audio-reactive glow.

8. **What should the share caption say?**
   "Pabandi just leveled up PropTech. ZK Proof of rent and tokenized yield streams on Solana. Your rent just became an asset."

9. **What's the user flow worth showing?**
   Upload rent payment → ZK Proof generated → Yield distributed on-chain.

## Storyboard (Cinematic Tone)
- **Scene 1 (0-3s):** Glowing text on dark background: "Rent used to be a black hole."
- **Scene 2 (3-7s):** "Now, it's an asset." (Glassmorphism card drops in showing USDC -> USDY swap).
- **Scene 3 (7-13s):** ZK Proof generation visual (abstract nodes connecting). Text: "Zero-Knowledge Proof of Rent. Private, Portable, Powerful."
- **Scene 4 (13-17s):** Split screen 50/50. "Yield split automatically between you and your landlord."
- **Scene 5 (17-20s):** Logo lockup. "Pabandi. Commitment, Secured on Web3."
