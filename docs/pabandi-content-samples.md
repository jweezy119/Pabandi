## uncleStory thread (sample)

1. My Delhi-uncle used to wire money via Western Union like it's 2007. Uncle, I said —
   yeh fees toh meri biwi bhi maanti thi jab Wi-Fi nahi tha.
2. The "bhaiya" he trusted? Ghosted after the first installment. Phone off, like
   Delhi-summer power cut. Uncle: "Par wo toh Cousin-Rajneesh's friend's nephew tha."
3. Pabandi = uss bouncer jaisa hai jo shaadiwale venue mein bana hota hai: paise release
   tabhi hote hain jab kaam theekh ho jaye. Trust score pe basis pe, not "rishtey mein hai."
4. Uncle: "Lekin beta, yeh token kya hota hai?" Dadi ke laddoo ki tarah socha — ek katori
   mein rakhte ho, dekhte raho. Kuch burn hote hain (damaged laddoo wala hissa), baki
   liquidity + yield mein jaata hai. Halal, no-ghost.
5. $PAB tradeable hai — matlab dadi ka laddo koi fridge mein nahi chhod deti. Tumhe pata
   hota hai kab nikal sakte ho. Par main kaam aane wala hoon, not a flipping game.
6. Ab bhaiya ko Western Union nahi, Pabandi bhejna padta hai. #DesiTech

## tokenomicsExplain (sample)

Dadi ke ghar ka budget samajh lo: jab bhi koi laddoo kharida, dadi usse teen
katoriya mein baant deti hai — jisme se ek katora jala deti hai (burn, taaki
koi future-mein maggie jaisa free mein nahi le sake), dusra liquidity rakh
ti hai (taki tumhare paise Mumbai-local-train jaisi jagah pe stuck na ho),
tisra yield mein jaata hai (halal interest, no-ghost), aur ek emergency
katora (maa-ke-pet-meina-paisa-rakhna wala fund). $PAB tradeable hai —
matlab dadi tumhe jab bhi chaiye, laddo nikal ke dogi. Par yeh nahi hai
"flip karo aur bhago," yeh hai "kaam aane wala fund."

Closer: "Paisa barbaad mat karo, beta — Pabandi rakhega safe, jaise dadi
rakhthi thi uss laddoo ko."
