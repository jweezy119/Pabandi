# Pabandi Growth Partners — Account Manager Agreement

This Account Manager Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between:

**Pabandi** ("Platform"), a business operating the Pabandi booking and escrow platform, and  
**[Account Manager Name / Handle]** ("Contractor"), an independent contractor.

---

## 1. Engagement
Platform engages Contractor as an independent contractor to onboard new businesses and users to the Platform. Contractor is not an employee, agent, or partner of Platform. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment relationship.

## 2. Term
This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until terminated by either party with **30 days written notice**, or immediately for Cause.

## 3. Duties
Contractor agrees to:
- Solicit and onboard new businesses and users using a unique referral link or code.
- Represent the Platform professionally and accurately.
- Complete any required onboarding documentation for referred accounts.
- Maintain compliance with Platform policies and applicable law.

## 4. Compensation
### 4.1 Program Rules
All compensation is earned only from **verified, fully paid, and completed bookings**. The full qualifying rules are:

1. Reservation status is `COMPLETED`.
2. Payment/deposit status is `PAID` or equivalent cleared state.
3. Booking is **not** `NO_SHOW`, `CANCELLED`, `REFUNDED`, or `FRAUD`.
4. Neither merchant nor buyer is under active fraud review at the time of payout calculation.
5. Contractor was the referring source at sign-up.

### 4.2 Sign-Up Bounty
- **$5.00 USD equivalent** per verified merchant account.
- “Verified” = business profile complete, first payment method onboarded, admin email confirmed.
- Paid once per business.

### 4.3 Recurring Booking Commission
- **3% of Platform Escrow Commission** per qualifying booking generated by the referred merchant or referred customer.
- Paid on the Platform’s fee only, not on the total transaction value.
- Decay schedule:
  - Year 1: 3%
  - Year 2: 2%
  - Year 3+: 1%
- Decay is calculated from the `createdAt` timestamp of the referred Business or User.

### 4.4 Caps
- **Per-merchant monthly cap:** $250.00/month in payout value.
- **Global partner program monthly cap:** $5,000.00/month across all partners.
- If a proposed payout would exceed either cap, the excess is forfeited and not carried forward.

### 4.5 Payout Method
- Payouts are issued in USD equivalent or Platform credit, at Platform’s discretion.
- Contractor must maintain a valid payout destination (bank transfer, mobile wallet, or Platform wallet).
- Minimum payout threshold may be set by Platform and updated with notice.

## 5. Prohibited Conduct
Contractor shall not:
- Create fake accounts, fake bookings, or self-referrals.
- Manipulate or abuse referral mechanics for personal gain.
- Use deceptive marketing or make false promises about the Platform.
- Poach onboarded merchants or users for a direct competitor during or after the term.
- Damage the Platform’s brand, reputation, or user trust.
- Violate any applicable law, including spam, privacy, or consumer protection rules.

## 6. Termination for Cause
Platform may terminate this Agreement immediately without notice if Contractor:
- Commits fraud, spam, or incentive manipulation.
- Damages the Platform’s reputation.
- Joins a direct competitor and attempts to poach referred merchants or users.
- Breaches confidentiality or prohibited conduct rules.

Upon termination for Cause:
- All future commissions are forfeited.
- Pending commissions are reversed.
- Contractor must repay any clawback amounts within **14 days**.

## 7. Clawback & Recoupment
If a payout was issued and later discovered to be tied to fraud, chargeback, or refund, Platform may:
- Reverse the payout.
- Deduct the amount from future payouts, or
- Demand repayment within 14 days.

The clawback window is **90 days** from the original payout date.

## 8. Confidentiality
Contractor agrees not to disclose Platform trade secrets, user data, pricing, or roadmap information to third parties without prior written consent.

## 9. Independent Contractor Status
- Contractor is responsible for all taxes, insurance, and benefits.
- Contractor may work for other platforms unless explicitly restricted in writing.
- Platform does not provide equipment, benefits, or payroll tax withholdings.

## 10. No Guarantee of Income
Platform makes no guarantee of earnings. Compensation depends entirely on verified, completed bookings generated by Contractor’s referrals.

## 11. Dispute Resolution
- Governing law: [Insert jurisdiction, e.g., Pakistan / UAE / Delaware, depending on Platform entity]
- Disputes shall be resolved by binding arbitration or small claims court where allowed.
- No class-action waiver that would be unconscionable under local law.

## 12. Entire Agreement
This Agreement supersedes all prior discussions. Changes must be in writing and signed by both parties.
