## Live Demo Script (2 Minutes)

### [0:00-0:15] The Hook
"Every year, no-shows cost small businesses billions. A restaurant with 20 tables loses $50/seat when someone doesn't show. Customers have no skin in the game. Pabandi fixes that."

### [0:15-0:45] Sitara OS — Discovery & Booking
"Open Pabandi. See restaurants on a map. Book a table at your favorite spot. Pay a $25 deposit — via card or USDC. The money is held in escrow, not by Pabandi, by a smart contract. You check in at the restaurant. The deposit releases to the business. You earn Star Power for showing up."

### [0:45-1:15] Mudarabah — Profit Sharing
"Businesses need capital. They create investment pools. AI matches investors to opportunities. You invest $1,000 in a local cafe. The cafe earns revenue. Profits are shared — 70% to you, 30% to the cafe. All on-chain. All transparent. No interest. No speculation. Real business value."

### [1:15-1:45] Property CRM
"Landlords manage 12 units. Screen tenants for free. Track rent, maintenance, deposits. Tenants get a white-label portal. No more spreadsheets."

### [1:45-2:00] Close
"Pabandi: Commitment, Secured. No business verification. No Stripe. Just Solana + USDC + trust."
