# Pabandi Account Manager Referral & Commission Plan
*Documented program design for founder review and approval*

## 1. Purpose
Pabandi needs verified sign-ups, not just raw registrations. This program turns trusted operators into accountable distribution channels by rewarding them only for **verified, fully paid, and completed bookings**. The goal is sustainable unit economics: every payout is tied to cleared revenue, not vanity metrics.

## 2. Program Name
**Pabandi Growth Partners**  
Internal alias: Account Manager Referral (`am_ref`).

## 3. Core Rules That Protect Pabandi
1. **Payout trigger = verified completed booking only.**  
   A booking must reach a terminal paid state before any commission is calculated or accrued. Pending, failed, refunded, or cancelled bookings generate zero commission.
2. **Commission comes from platform revenue, not merchant or buyer gross.**  
   We pay Growth Partners from the platform fee slice, preserving booking value for both sides.
3. **No unlimited uncapped exposure.**  
   Every partner has a hard monthly payout cap and a per-booking cap.
4. **Fraud = immediate forfeiture + clawback.**  
   Self-referrals, fake accounts, fake bookings, incentive manipulation, or brand damage trigger contract termination and reversal of pending/unpaid commissions.
5. **Contractor status, not employment.**  
   Partners are independent contractors. No benefits, no implied employment, no exclusivity.
6. **30-day notice + cause-based forfeiture.**  
   Either side can terminate with 30 days. If a partner breaches the code of conduct, joins a direct competitor with intent to poach, or commits fraud, all future and pending commissions are forfeited.

## 4. Incentive Structure
### 4.1 Sign-up Bounty (One-Time)
- **$5.00 equivalent** in $PAB or platform credit per verified merchant account.  
- “Verified” = business profile completed, first payment method onboarded, admin email confirmed.
- Paid once per business, not per user.

### 4.2 Ongoing Booking Fee Share (Recurring)
- **3% of platform escrow commission** for every qualifying booking generated by the referred merchant or referred customer.
- Calculated on the platform’s take only, not on the total transaction.

This is safer than “% of total booking” because:
- Booking values vary.
- Platform margins are thin; harvesting from the top line risks unprofitability.
- It keeps the math auditable: `commission = platform_fee * 0.10`.

### 4.3 Decay / Cap Rules (Recommended)
- **Year 1:** 3% of platform fee.  
- **Year 2:** 2% of platform fee.  
- **Year 3+:** 1% of platform fee.  
- **Per-merchant monthly cap:** $250/month in payout value.  
- **Global partner cap:** $5,000/month across all partners.

Caps prevent a single viral partner from bankrupting the program.

## 5. Qualified Booking Definition
A booking is commission-qualifying only if ALL of the following are true:
1. Reservation status is `COMPLETED`.
2. Deposit/payment status is `PAID` or equivalent verified-cleared state.
3. Booking is **not** marked `NO_SHOW`, `CANCELLED`, `REFUNDED`, or `FRAUD`.
4. Neither the merchant nor the buyer is flagged in the current fraud review.
5. The partner was the referring source at the time of sign-up, not retroactively changed.

## 6. Technical Implementation
### 6.1 Database Changes
- `User.referredById` — already exists. Use it for buyer-side attribution.
- `Business.referredById` — new field. Use it for merchant-side attribution.
- `AccountManagerProfile` model — stores partner metadata, wallet, status, caps.
- `ReferralLedger` model — immutable entry per payout-eligible event.
- `ReferralPayout` model — tracks settled vs reversed payouts.

### 6.2 Backend Logic
1. **Attribution at sign-up**  
   Sign-up flow accepts a `?ref=<partnerCode>` parameter and writes `referredById` to the new `User` and/or `Business` record.

2. **Qualification at booking terminal state**  
   On every terminal booking state transition, the backend:
   - Reads the merchant’s `referredById`.
   - Reads the buyer’s `referredById`.
   - Identifies the associated `AccountManagerProfile`.
   - Checks partner status (`ACTIVE`), caps, and fraud flags.
   - Calculates `platformFee * rate`.
   - Writes an immutable `ReferralLedger` entry.

3. **Settlement**  
   - Batch settlement runs nightly.
   - Payouts accumulate in `ReferralLedger.unsettledAmount`.
   - Once `ReferralPayout` is created, the ledger entry is marked `SETTLED`.
   - If later reversed (chargeback / fraud), the payout moves to `REVERSED` and the amount is deducted from future available payout or marked as clawback if already sent.

4. **Partner Dashboard API**  
   - GET `/account-manager/summary` — total referrals, qualified bookings, pending payout, settled payout.
   - GET `/account-manager/referrals` — list of referred merchants/users with status.
   - POST `/account-manager/payout/request` — request payout if above minimum threshold.
   - GET `/account-manager/ledger` — immutable ledger history with status.

### 6.3 Frontend / UX
- **Partner Invite Page** (`/account-manager/invite`) — one-tap copy of unique referral link.
- **Dashboard** (`/account-manager/dashboard`) — cards for sign-ups, qualified bookings, pending vs settled payout, trust health of onboarded merchants.
- **Onboarding flow** — short explainer with expected earnings, caps, and rules before issuing partner credentials.

## 7. Smart Contract Considerations (Phase 2)
Initially, store everything off-chain in Prisma for speed and auditability.
Once volume is proven, move allocation logic to a Solana program or existing on-chain accounting:
- Immutable ledger hashes.
- Partner claims via wallet signature.
- On-chain payout splitter sends USDC/$PAB directly to partner wallet.

This preserves compliance while adding trustless settlement later.

## 8. Partner Agreement Draft
### 8.1 Clauses That Must Exist
- **Independent Contractor.** No employee rights, no benefits, no exclusivity.
- **Compensation.** 3% of platform escrow commission on verified completed bookings, subject to caps and decay schedule.
- **Qualifying Event Definition.** Exact list from Section 5.
- **Termination for Cause.** Fraud, brand damage, competitor poaching, fake accounts, fake bookings.
- **Clawback Rights.** If payout was issued and later reversed due to fraud or chargeback, partner must repay within 14 days or it is offset against future payouts.
- **Dispute Resolution.** Arbitration-friendly jurisdiction; no class-action waiver that would be unconscionable in the partner’s home country.
- **IP / Confidentiality.** Partner cannot use Pabandi trademarks in misleading contexts.
- **Governing Law.** Specify jurisdiction; example: Pakistan or UAE business-friendly zone if that matches ops.

### 8.2 Explicitly Excluded
- Never guarantee income.
- Never describe the relationship as employment.
- Never allow partners to represent “official Pabandi staff.”

## 9. Anti-Fraud Protections
1. **Rate limit guest bookings** from a single IP or device fingerprint.
2. **Detect cyclical self-referrals** (A refers B, B refers A).
3. **Velocity checks** — maximum N new referred businesses per partner per week.
4. **Manual review queue** for first 10 bookings per new merchant.
5. **Clawback window** — 90 days after payout, if fraud is discovered.
6. **Wallet reputation** — partner wallets are checked against known abuse patterns before activation.
7. **KYC threshold** — partners above $500/month total payout trigger KYC or document verification.

## 10. Financial Model Sample
Assume:
- 100 merchants onboarded by partners in Month 1.
- Each averages 20 completed bookings/month.
- Average booking value $80.
- Platform commission = 1% of booking value.
- Partner rate = 3% of platform fee = 0.03% of booking value.

Revenue per merchant/month = 20 * $80 * 0.01 = **$16**  
Payout per merchant/month = $16 * 0.03 = **$0.48**  
Total partner payout Month 1 = 100 * $0.48 = **$48**

Even if volume is 10x, payouts stay under **$1,600/month**, well within startup burn tolerance.

With the $5 sign-up bounty:
100 merchants * $5 = **$500 one-time**

This is cheap customer acquisition with lifetime attribution.

## 11. Rollout Sequence
1. **Week 1:** Schema changes + backend ledger service + admin-only partner creation.
2. **Week 2:** Partner dashboard + invite link flow + migration SQL.
3. **Week 3:** Fraud checks + nightly settlement job + partner agreement draft.
4. **Week 4:** Closed beta with 5–10 trusted partners + dashboard refinement.
5. **Week 6:** Public launch with cap enforcement and monitoring.

## 13. Success Metrics
- Verified merchant rate: >70% of referred accounts complete onboarding.
- Qualified booking rate: >40% of referred sign-ups complete a paid booking within 30 days.
- Fraud rate: <2% of ledger entries reversed.
- Payout latency: <48 hours after settlement window.
- Partner NPS: >40 within first 30 days of beta.

## 14. Summary
The Pabandi Growth Partners program is intentionally conservative:
- It rewards real revenue, not hype.
- It protects margins with platform-fee-only math and hard caps.
- It deters fraud through immutable ledgers, clawbacks, and KYC triggers.
- It aligns with existing Pabandi trust infrastructure because partners are evaluated partly by the merchant health they onboard.

This is a distribution moat, not an affiliate scheme.
