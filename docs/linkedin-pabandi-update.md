# LinkedIn Content — Pabandi Update Post

## Post Copy

Most booking platforms still treat trust as an afterthought.

We’re changing that with three core upgrades now live in Pabandi:

1. **Dynamic AI Escrow (DAE)** — deposit amounts adapt in real time based on risk signals, not rigid flat rates. Lower friction for reliable sellers. Smarter protection for buyers.

2. **Proof-of-Presence (PoP)** — buyers and merchants can mark intent and arrival inside checkout. That turns trust into an auditable event chain, not a static review.

3. **TrustKeyring & Passport Risk Engine** — wallet attestations, social-graph signals, and Web3 stakes feed a live trust score that updates escrow terms automatically.

None of this is theory. It’s wired into checkout, mounted on Cloud Run, and shipping to mobile today.

The broader bet: the next wave of commerce won’t be “Web2 vs Web3.” It will be whoever makes trust programmable, transparent, and mobile-native first.

We’re building that layer for bookings — starting with Pakistan, built for everywhere.

If you’re working on escrow, reputation, or Web3 UX, I’d love to hear what you’re shipping.
