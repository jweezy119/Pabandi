# Brag Plan: Pabandi Growth Partners — Account Manager Incentives

## What is this app?
Pabandi's Account Manager program turns your personal network into a passive income engine: refer businesses or customers, earn a $5 PAB signup bounty per referral and lifetime decaying commissions (3% → 2% → 1%) on every booking your referrals generate — forever.

## The angle
This isn't a generic "refer a friend" promo. This is a recruitment pitch that makes the viewer feel like they're already losing money by NOT being a Growth Partner. The numbers do the talking: real commission rates pulled from the live codebase, real crypto payouts, a real dashboard tracking every dollar. The hook is the compound effect — one referral code, dozens of businesses, thousands of bookings, all feeding your wallet while you sleep.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A single dramatic stat — "$0.00" on a dark screen. Then it starts counting up. Fast. Relentlessly. The question isn't IF you'll earn — it's how much you're missing right now.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The Commission Engine** — Three tier cards slam in: "Year 1: 3%" → "Year 2: 2%" → "Year 3+: 1% forever". The word "forever" lingers.
- **The Dashboard in Action** — The actual Partner Dashboard UI: stat cards (Total Earned, Active Businesses, Total Bookings, Escrow Volume) counting up with real-looking numbers. Lead scoring bars filling.
- **The Referral Link** — A glowing referral URL `pabandi.com/register?ref=YOUR_CODE` being copied, shared. Each share triggers a bounty: "+$5 PAB" stacking up.
- **Dual Revenue Streams** — Split screen showing a business signing up (bounty hit) on one side and a customer booking (commission hit) on the other. Both feed the same wallet.

## Outro / punchline
The total earned counter freezes on a large number. Below it: "Your network. Your income. Forever." Then the Pabandi logo with "Become a Growth Partner" CTA.

## User flow worth showing
Activate Partner Account (one-click button) → Unique referral link generated → Share link → Business signs up via your code → Business gets bookings → Commission auto-deposited to your wallet → Payout ledger updates in real-time.

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: "high-stakes recruitment pitch — the money counter never lies"
- Interpretation: Dramatic reveals, big typography, dark moody palette. Each number hit lands with weight. Restraint in animation density but maximum impact per beat. The pacing builds tension — slow reveal, then overwhelming proof.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 25 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #080e17 (near-black navy)
- Accent: #14f195 (Solana green — used for money/success)
- Primary: #3b82f6 (Pabandi blue)
- Gold: #f0b429 (PAB token gold — used in rewards UI)
- Text: #e8edf2 (soft white)
- Muted text: #94a3b8 (slate)
- Display font: Manrope (800 weight for headlines)
- Body font: Inter (400-600 for labels)
- Strongest visual elements: The Partner Dashboard stat cards with rounded-3xl corners, glowing borders (green→blue→purple gradient), the dark surface layering system (#05090f → #0b121e → #0f172a → #1e293b)

## Share copy (draft)
Pabandi Growth Partners: refer one business, earn 3% on every booking they ever get. Lifetime commissions. Real $PAB crypto payouts. Your network is literally your income.

## Audio direction
- Role: cinematic support — building energy, climactic reveal
- Music: dark cinematic electronic bed with a low bass pulse and building synth layers; think "startup pitch meets heist film"
- Music treatment: starts minimal (bass pulse only) at hook, layers build through commission tiers, full energy at dashboard reveal, gentle resolution at outro
- Music cue guidance: to be detected at composition time; target strong cues at ~2s (counter starts), ~8s (commission tiers slam), ~16s (dashboard full reveal), ~23s (logo hit)
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; use bass energy to pulse glow on stat cards, slight scale breathe on the counter
- SFX posture: moderate; motion-matched; professional restraint
- Audio-coupled moments: counter ticking up (subtle digital tick), commission tier cards slamming in (impact whoosh), "+$5 PAB" bounty hits (cash register chime), referral link copy (clipboard snap), final logo (deep resonant hit)
- Restraint rule: audio must never feel like a mobile game ad — no constant chimes, no cheesy fanfares. Every sound earns its place.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The Counter Hook — 3s
Dark screen (#080e17). A single number in massive Manrope 800 type: "$0.00" in #14f195 green. After 0.5s beat, it starts counting up rapidly — $0.12… $3.47… $18.92… $147.83… numbers blur as they accelerate. Subtle text below in #94a3b8: "Your referral earnings. Right now."
Sequential/interaction: yes — counter animates up with accelerating speed, numbers blur at peak velocity
Audio intent: intrigue, then growing urgency as the counter accelerates
Audio-coupled idea: digital tick sound accelerating with the counter, pitch rising slightly
Music: minimal bass pulse, single synth note builds
Transition mood: dramatic → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The Commission Tiers — 5s
Three large tier cards slam in from below, one by one (staggered 0.6s apart). Each card is a rounded-2xl dark surface (#0f172a) with glowing border. Card 1: "YEAR 1" label in #94a3b8, "3%" in massive #3b82f6. Card 2: "YEAR 2", "2%" in #3b82f6. Card 3: "YEAR 3+", "1%" in #3b82f6 with a small "forever" tag in #14f195 that pulses once. Below all three: "of every booking's platform fee" in #94a3b8.
Sequential/interaction: yes — three cards arrive one by one from below with slight overshoot ease, "forever" tag appears after cards settle
Audio intent: powerful, each card landing with weight
Audio-coupled idea: each card slam gets a low-frequency impact hit; "forever" gets a distinct resonant chime
Music: building — second synth layer enters, bass deepens
Transition mood: clean → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — The Bounty Stack — 4s
Split-composition. Left side: a simplified business registration form (dark card, business name field, "Sign Up" button) with cursor clicking "Register" — immediately a "+$5 PAB" badge flies up from the button in #f0b429 gold. Right side: a simplified customer booking card ("Booking Confirmed ✓") — immediately "+3% Commission" floats up in #14f195. Both sides feed a central wallet balance counter at bottom that increments with each hit. The referral link URL `pabandi.com/register?ref=AX7F3E92` glows in #3b82f6 across the top.
Sequential/interaction: yes — left side fires first (business signup + bounty), right side fires 0.8s later (customer booking + commission), wallet balance updates with each
Audio intent: satisfying reward sounds, building momentum
Audio-coupled idea: business click gets a tap sound + cash register chime on bounty; booking confirm gets a subtle ding + commission whoosh; wallet update gets a soft tally tick
Music: energy builds, rhythm becomes more defined
Transition mood: dramatic → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — The Live Dashboard — 6s
Full recreation of the Partner Dashboard UI. Dark (#05090f) background. Five stat cards appear one by one across the top: "Total Earned: $2,847.50" (green icon), "Escrow Vol: $14,237.50" (purple icon), "Pending Payouts: $312.00" (tertiary icon), "Active Businesses: 23" (blue icon), "Total Bookings: 487" (secondary icon). Below: two panels side by side — left shows "Referred Businesses & Lead Scoring" with 3 business rows (each with name, verification badge, booking count, lead score bar filling to 85-95%). Right shows "Recent Earnings" ledger with entries like "+$12.40 booking commission" and "+$5.00 signup bounty" in green.
Sequential/interaction: yes — stat cards stagger in (0.4s apart), each counter does a fast count-up to its final value; then the two lower panels fade in together; lead score bars animate fill from 0% to their values
Audio intent: climactic — this is the payoff, the proof
Audio-coupled idea: each stat card gets a subtle pop on arrival; counters tick rapidly; lead score bars get a soft fill swoosh
Music: full energy, all layers present, peak moment
Transition mood: soft → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — The Punchline & CTA — 4s
Counter from Scene 1 returns, but now frozen on "$2,847.50" in large #14f195 type. Below it, three lines fade in sequentially (0.6s apart): "Your network." in #e8edf2, "Your income." in #e8edf2, "Forever." in #14f195 with a subtle glow pulse. After a 1s hold, the Pabandi logo (from /public/pab-logo.png) fades in below with "Become a Growth Partner →" CTA button styled in the app's primary gradient (from-primary to-primary-container).
Sequential/interaction: yes — three text lines arrive one by one; logo and CTA fade in together after the final line
Audio intent: resolution, confidence, weight on "Forever"
Audio-coupled idea: each text line gets silence (let the words land); logo hit gets a deep, satisfying final impact
Music: resolves — final chord sustains, gentle fade

**Music mood for this video:** cinematic electronic — dark, building, climactic
**Audio summary:** Starts with a lone bass pulse under a counting hook, builds through impact hits on commission tier reveals and bounty animations, peaks at the full dashboard display with layered synth energy, then resolves to a sustained chord under the "Forever" punchline and logo hit.
