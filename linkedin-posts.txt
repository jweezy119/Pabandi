--- POST 3: PABANDI x WEBACY (SOLANA AI-AGENT SAFETY & ESCROW) ---

AI agents can already browse, book, and pay.
What they still lack is a full-stack trust layer before funds leave a wallet.

Today we shipped a live integration between Pabandi (پابندی) and @Webacy on Solana:

• On-chain safety (Webacy): Real-time Solana wallet threat scoring, KYW checks, and sanctions screening.
• Behavioral trust & settlement (Pabandi): Portable Trust Passports, non-custodial Solana Anchor escrow, and MCP tooling for autonomous agents.

What's live in production:
🔒 Automatic hard-blocking at our Solana escrow oracle for sanctioned or high-risk wallets (>= 75/100)
🛂 Live on-chain risk signals surfaced directly inside Pabandi Trust Passports
🤖 New `pabandi_screen_wallet` MCP tool + rate-limited REST endpoint with 15-min caching
