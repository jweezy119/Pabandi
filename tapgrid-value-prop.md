# TapGrid × Pabandi: Turn Every Deployed Tag Into a Checkout

## The one-line pitch
Your tags already get tapped every day. Today each tap earns TapGrid $0 after the hardware sale. We make the tap take payment — and split what it earns.

## Why this works with what exists right now
- **No new hardware.** Your deployed NFC tags are URL pointers. We reprogram a handful to point at Pabandi TapPay checkout links instead of profile pages. Same tags, same phones, new behavior.
- **No new Pabandi build.** Payment links, escrow deposits, verified check-in, and review-to-star flow are live in production today.
- **No money changes hands to start.** The pilot costs TapGrid five reprogrammed tags and five merchant intros. It costs Pabandi the onboarding calls.

## The pilot (30 days, 3–5 friendly merchants)
1. Pick merchants with repeat foot traffic (coffee, salon, fast-casual).
2. Reprogram one tag per location: tap → menu/order → pay deposit → receipt.
3. Merchant keeps their current POS. We sit alongside it, handling deposits, no-show protection, and verified reviews — the three things their POS doesn't do.
4. Measure: taps, tap-to-paid conversion, deposit dollars protected, verified reviews earned.

## What the merchant gets (their words, not ours)
- Deposits that actually show up — no-shows stop being a cost of doing business.
- Verified reviews from real check-ins, which rank them higher in discovery.
- No new terminal, no new monthly software, no staff retraining. One tag.

## What TapGrid gets
- **A second revenue line** on hardware already sold: share of processing on every tap-transaction, forever — no manufacturing, no inventory.
- **A churn shield.** A tag that takes payments never gets peeled off the counter. A tag that opens a profile does.
- **Proof for every future sale.** "This tag processed $X last month" outsells any spec sheet.

## What Pabandi gets
- Distribution we can't buy: real merchants, real foot traffic, real transactions.
- Verified users and businesses feeding the star economy from day one.

## Proposed commercial terms (pilot)
- Non-exclusive, 30 days, cancellable by either side with 7 days' notice.
- TapGrid keeps 100% of existing hardware/SaaS revenue. New transaction revenue splits 70/30 Pabandi/TapGrid during pilot, renegotiated on real volume.
- Each side owns its IP. Joint case study requires both signatures.
- No exclusivity, no joint entity, no upfront fees. Paperwork fits on two pages.

## The ask
Five merchant names. Permission to reprogram five tags. Thirty days. If taps don't convert, we shake hands and you keep everything we learned.
