## Video direction

- **palette system**: `blue-professional`
- **motion grammar**: smooth power3 reveals, paced to the VO. At t=0 only what the VO is saying enters. No front-loading.
- **rhythm**: active build-ups in scenes 1-4, held read in scene 5 (before the turn), active payoff in scene 6.
- **negative list**: no screen captures, no purple-blue AI gradients, no slideshow dumping, no idle breathing.
