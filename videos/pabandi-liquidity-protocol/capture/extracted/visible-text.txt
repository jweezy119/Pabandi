1. The On-Ramp: Customer Pays (Cash / PKR)
A customer in Lahore books a salon appointment requiring a 5,000 PKR deposit.
They check out using their standard Meezan Bank or HBL debit card. We process this transaction using our local payment gateway integration (Safepay).
Result: The customer's bank account is debited 5,000 PKR. To the customer, this was just a normal online purchase.

2. The Tokenization: Fiat to Crypto (USDC)
As soon as Safepay successfully charges the card, the fiat settles into Pabandi's local acquiring account.
In exactly the same millisecond, the Pabandi FX Router kicks in. It takes an equivalent amount of USDC (let's say $18 USDC) from Pabandi's own crypto treasury and locks it into a Smart Contract Escrow on Solana on behalf of the customer.
Result: The 5,000 PKR fiat is safely in the bank, and it is now represented dynamically on the blockchain as $18 USDC sitting in escrow.

3. The Wait: Escrow & Yield
While waiting a few days for the actual appointment to happen, that $18 USDC doesn't just sit there.
It is pushed into the Validation-as-a-Service (VaaS) engine, earning staking yields on Solana.

4. The Release: Service Completed
The customer shows up and gets their haircut. The booking is marked as honored.
The smart contract officially unlocks the $18 USDC from the escrow vault and credits it to the Merchant's digital Pabandi Balance.

5. The Off-Ramp: Crypto to Merchant (Cash / PKR)
The salon owner looks at their Pabandi app and sees they have funds available to withdraw. They click "Withdraw to Raast".
This triggers the Pabandi Liquidity Protocol (PLP). The system broadcasts to our network of local Liquidity Providers (market makers).
A local Liquidity Provider (LP) accepts the match. The LP wants the $18 USDC.
To get it, the LP opens their local banking app and instantly sends 5,000 PKR via Raast directly to the salon owner's IBAN or mobile number.
Once the Raast transaction is confirmed, Pabandi releases the $18 USDC on-chain to the LP's Phantom wallet.

The Final Result
The Customer: Paid 5,000 PKR from their bank account.
The Merchant: Received 5,000 PKR instantly in their local bank account via Raast.
The Middle (Pabandi): Used crypto rails (Solana, USDC, and Smart Contracts) to secure the funds in escrow, generate yield, and instantly settle cross-border liquidity without ever relying on the slow, expensive SWIFT banking network!
